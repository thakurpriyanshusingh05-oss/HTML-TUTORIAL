Unprecedented Scale

Cricket's pulse was felt everywhere.

The 2023 edition was a digital and physical powerhouse, smashing records across the globe. From packed stadiums to concurrent streaming peaks, the world had never witnessed cricket at this scale before.

Hosted entirely in India for the first time, the tournament captured the imagination of a nation — and the entire cricketing world followed along.

The Colosseum of Cricket

Narendra Modi Stadium

Ahmedabad · Grand Opening & The Final

The world's largest cricket stadium roared with 132,000 voices as it hosted both the grand opening ceremony and the climactic final — a fitting home for one of cricket's most unforgettable spectacles.

A sea of blue filled every tier as India marched through the tournament undefeated, culminating in the ultimate showdown on this hallowed ground.

Tournament Dominance

The Indian Juggernaut

10-Win Streak

India marched into the final undefeated, showcasing a lethal blend of batting brilliance and bowling mastery that crushed every opponent in their path.

Shami's Fury

The highest wicket-taker of the tournament with 24 wickets, including a record-breaking 7-fer in the semi-final. Mohammed Shami was unstoppable when it mattered most.

Clinical Defense

Team India's bowling unit defended modest totals with ease, silencing opposition batters across every venue and condition encountered on the subcontinent.

The King's Landmark

Virat Kohli:
50th Century

At the Wankhede Stadium, the world witnessed history as Virat Kohli surpassed Sachin Tendulkar's legendary record of 49 ODI centuries — a moment 15 years in the making.

With 765 runs in a single edition, he cemented his status as the "Greatest of the Era," winning the Player of the Tournament award in dominant fashion.

The diving catch that changed everything
The Catch that Changed it All

Rohit Sharma was on a rampage, threatening a massive total. In a moment of pure athleticism, Travis Head sprinted 18 meters to pluck a stunning diving catch.

This dismissal broke India's momentum and silenced a stadium of 100,000+ voices — setting the stage for Australia's tactical takeover of the biggest final in ODI history.

After a shaky start at 47/3, Travis Head played one of the most calculated yet aggressive knocks in World Cup final history.
