01 Opening Ceremony · October 5

The Grand Curtain Raiser

The 13th edition of the Cricket World Cup kicked off with a spectacular opening ceremony at the Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad — the world's largest cricket ground. 10 nations marched in under the blazing lights as billions tuned in globally, heralding the start of cricket's greatest festival.

02 India's Unstoppable Run Begins

India's Batting Masterclass

Rohit Sharma led India to a stunning 428/5 against Afghanistan, signalling the host nation's intent. Virat Kohli compiled a masterful innings while Rohit himself smashed a blistering half-century from just 28 balls. India would go unbeaten throughout the group stage, winning all 9 matches.

9 Consecutive Group Stage Wins
03 October 15 · El Clasico of Cricket

India vs Pakistan — The Billion-Dollar Game

The most anticipated match in world cricket did not disappoint. Before a packed Narendra Modi Stadium and 1.4 billion eyes on TV, India defeated Pakistan in a nail-biting thriller. Rohit Sharma's blazing century set the platform before Siraj and Bumrah dismantled Pakistan's chase. Cricket's greatest rivalry delivered once more.

IND won by 7 wickets

04 November 2 · History Rewritten

Kohli's 50th ODI Century

In one of sport's most emotional moments, Virat Kohli scored his 50th ODI century against South Africa at Wankhede Stadium, surpassing Sachin Tendulkar's legendary record of 49. The crowd erupted as Kohli raised his bat — tears, cheers, and history all at once. Kohli ended the tournament as the highest run-scorer with 765 runs.

765 Runs in the Tournament
05 Semi-Final · November 15

India's Dominant Semi-Final

India dismantled New Zealand in the semi-final at Wankhede Stadium with a comprehensive performance. Shami's devastating spell of 7/57 — the best bowling figures in World Cup knockout history — ripped through the New Zealand lineup. Rohit Sharma's breezy 47 and Shreyas Iyer's half-century set a formidable target as India marched into the final unbeaten.

06 Semi-Final · November 16

Australia's Great Escape

Unbeaten South Africa entered as one of the tournament's most impressive sides, but Australia — as they have done so often — found a way. In a gripping chase at Eden Gardens, David Warner and Mitchell Marsh's partnership turned the match. Australia barely scraped through by 3 wickets, setting up a dream final against the host nation India.
